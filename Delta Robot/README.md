# Delta Robot

## 1. Project Title

**Design and Development of a Parallel Delta Robot for Precision Robotic Manipulation**

## 2. Short Description

Designed and developed a **parallel Delta robot** focusing on high-speed and precise robotic positioning. The project involved mechanical design, robotic kinematics, actuator integration, and coordinated motion control to demonstrate the capabilities of parallel robotic mechanisms for manipulation applications.

## 3. My Contribution

* Designed and developed the mechanical structure of the Delta robot.
* Analyzed the geometry and kinematics of the parallel robotic mechanism.
* Developed the mathematical model for robotic positioning and motion.
* Integrated actuators and motor-control electronics.
* Implemented coordinated actuator movement for end-effector positioning.
* Tested the robot's workspace, motion, and positioning performance.
* Troubleshot mechanical and control issues during system development.

## 4. Hardware / Components

* Delta Robot Mechanical Structure
* Stepper Motors
